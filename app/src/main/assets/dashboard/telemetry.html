Ride log
Record ride log
Off by default. Records the complete telemetry snapshot the scooter sends, once per minute while you ride: speeds, gear, power, currents, pack and pole voltage, motor temperatures, trip and total distance, status and error bits plus the whole battery block with every cell voltage. Recording starts as soon as the scooter first moves. All rides are kept until you delete them.
View the exported CSV/JSON as graphs with the Laufbursche Edition Analysis Tool (leat).

Firmware update
Read this first. Flashing IVCU firmware over Bluetooth takes about 13 minutes. During the update: keep the scooter switched on and within range, keep this app in the foreground (the screen stays on) and do not turn the scooter off. If the flash is interrupted the IVCU stays in update mode and can simply be flashed again. It is not bricked. This page flashes whatever .hex you pick, so only flash a file that matches your IVCU.
ver2 device (T2/tetra). This scooter flashes every node (display, light module, the front/rear controllers, BMS) through the display block, selecting the target node from the firmware file's header (a 06 e2 handshake). Pick the matching component's .hex and flash as usual; the node is chosen automatically from the file.
